# Ops playbook (Acturus)

Sources, both in the shared Drive folder (Content Creation > Content Planning area): **3D BORE Social Media Strategy** (5 Nov 2026) and **3D BORE Instagram Content Plan (Nov-Dec 2026)**. Everything below is a **proposal** from those documents. Do not present any of it as a decision. Source quality is uneven: some citations in the strategy doc are weak (for example the Ramadan row cites a Google Sports Data help page, and a FabLab claim cites a stock-photo page). Follower, revenue and machine-load numbers are targets, not forecasts. Verify before anything customer-facing.

## 1. Business shape
- Position: a precision engineering partner (3D printing with Bambu Lab A1, plus CNC), not a per-gram print farm. Marketplace print vendors compete on price (the doc cites Rp400 to Rp1,200 per gram for PLA+). 3D BORE's price is the owner's call.
- Two transaction paths: standard parts on a marketplace (Shop | Tokopedia, Shopee) with escrow; custom work through WhatsApp Business.
- Funnel: short video (discovery) > carousels (technical trust) > WhatsApp or marketplace (conversion).

## 2. Intake to order (proposed flow)
1. Link in bio gives two choices: marketplace shop, or "Konsultasi Cetak & CNC Kustom" to WhatsApp.
2. WhatsApp message is pre-filled with material (PLA, PETG, TPU, CNC), need (prototype, tugas akhir, machine part) and whether the file is ready. Keep the number as `[NOMOR WA]` until the owner confirms it.
3. Engineering review of the STEP or STL file: slicing check, weight, print time, cost breakdown.
4. Formal quote. The strategy doc proposes a **2 business hour** reply SLA. It is a proposal and only the owner can adopt it.
5. Payment by bank transfer or a marketplace listing with escrow, then production scheduling.
- Radical price transparency is proposed: publish the baseline formula (material, setup fee, machine time) so customers do not have to "DM buat harga". The numbers come from the owner.

## 3. Quality and delivery
- Check critical dimensions with calipers before shipping. Claim only tolerances that were measured.
- Complaint triggers to design out: price added after the quote, parts that do not assemble, slow WhatsApp replies.
- Single-machine risk: the workshop runs one Bambu Lab A1. The doc proposes keeping spares (hotend, extruder, belts) and a backup partner print shop, and planning a second unit when utilization passes **70%**. The 40% and 70% load figures and revenue ranges in the doc are targets, not facts.

## 4. Compliance (summary of the strategy doc; not legal advice, owner to confirm with a lawyer)
| Topic | What the doc says | Practical rule |
|---|---|---|
| Permendag 31/2023 | Social platforms may not process payments natively | Route final payment through a marketplace or documented business account. |
| Consumer protection (UU 8/1999) | State the limits of printed parts | Put a disclaimer on invoices and order forms. Proposed wording: "Komponen cetak 3D FDM ditujukan untuk pembuatan prototipe fungsional dan validasi desain. Pengujian kekuatan akhir untuk aplikasi berisiko tinggi berada di bawah tanggung jawab pemesan." |
| Personal data (UU PDP 27/2022) | Client CAD files are sensitive | Confidentiality pledge on every intake form. NDAs offered for commercial projects. Never show a customer file, name or photo without written permission. |
| Advertising ethics (EPI) | Sponsored posts must be labelled | Use #Iklan or #Endorse on any paid or barter collaboration. |
| Licenses | CC BY-NC designs cannot be sold as inventory | Check the license of every third-party file. Credit the designer on screen and in the caption. CC0 and CC BY may be fabricated for customers with credit. CC BY-SA needs the same license on changes. |
| Universal Disclaimer | From the BSG page 12 | On every manufacturing-result post: all IP and design rights belong to the creator or client, and 3D BORE provides manufacturing services only, shown with permission. |

## 5. Seasonal and business calendar (proposed, check dates)
- **Harbolnas twin dates:** 11.11 and 12.12. 11.11 is five days from this file's creation, so any marketplace listing or promo needs an owner decision now.
- **Gajian:** the 25th to the 2nd of each month. Spending rises, so schedule offers there.
- **Academic terms:** July to August and January (tugas akhir projects).
- **Kontes Robot Indonesia (KRI):** about April to July. Robotics teams need rugged parts fast.
- **Ramadan and Lebaran:** shifts yearly, roughly February to April in the doc's table. THR raises spending, and B2B pauses during Lebaran week. Check the actual 2027 dates.

## 6. Community and B2B (all need owner approval)
- Student discount (15% for verified tugas akhir or skripsi) and KRI team sponsorships with co-branded content. Discounts and sponsorships are prices and promos, which only the owner approves.
- Barter with nano and micro creators (1,000 to 50,000 followers) in exchange for parts. Label as #Endorse.
- Paid ads: boost proven organic posts only. The strategy doc suggests small boosts first.

## 7. Risk register seed
| Risk | Mitigation proposed in the doc |
|---|---|
| Marketplace price war | Sell engineering consultation and precision, not per-gram price. |
| One machine, single point of failure | Spares, backup shop, second unit trigger. |
| Followers do not start a WhatsApp chat | Test the link-in-bio, show price formulas, offer a free tolerance chip as a lead magnet. |
| Client file confidentiality worries | Pledge, NDA, UU PDP compliance. |
| License disputes | License check and credit on every third-party file. |
| Public complaints | Upfront pricing, caliper check, fast replies. |
